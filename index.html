Key Theorems:
• Comparison sorts: lower bound = Ω(n log n)
• Bucket/Radix/Counting sort beat this because they are non-comparison based
• Randomized QuickSort: expected O(n log n), worst case O(n²) but probability exponentially small
• Insertion sort optimal for k-sorted arrays: O(nk)
